### 4. VHS vocabulary upgrade — technical terms over aesthetic labels

**Applies to:** Problem #1 (grit intensification)

Replace:
- `heavy grain` → `magnetic noise, signal loss flashes`
- `VHS grain` → `chroma distortion, dropout artifacts, wobbling horizontal lines`
- `VHS style` → `authentic early-2000s surveillance footage, worn playback tape degradation`

The reasoning: GPT trained on text describing images. Text naming the physical mechanism may better activate the right training associations than aesthetic outcome labels.

**Confidence: MED** (2 independent sources; no direct ablation test on GPT)
